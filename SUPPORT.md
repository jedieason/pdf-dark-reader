# Support

For setup or usage questions, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/jedieason/pdf-dark-reader/issues) and describe your Chrome version, operating system, and what you expected to happen. For PDF-specific problems, attach a non-sensitive sample PDF or provide steps to create one. Please do not upload private documents.

For security vulnerabilities, use [private reporting](SECURITY.md) instead of a public issue.
